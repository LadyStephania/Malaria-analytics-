Model Methodology

This view computes a Pearson correlation coefficient (r) between the selected environmental driver and RDT-confirmed case counts, offset by the chosen lag window to approximate the mosquito breeding-to-clinical-presentation delay. Statistical significance is estimated via a Fisher z-transformation of r, converted to a two-tailed p-value against the sample size shown above. Adjust the driver and lag controls to explore how the relationship shifts across different environmental variables and time offsets.
